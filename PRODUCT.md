# Product

## Register

product

## Platform

web

## Users

Event operators and audience, with equal weight. Operators create sessions, go live, and share a link under event pressure. Audience members open the viewer on a phone or laptop and follow captions while listening. When the two surfaces conflict, optimize each for its own user rather than picking a global winner.

## Product Purpose

Echo provides live multilingual captions for talks and events. The speaker talks; the audience reads in their language. A good session feels frictionless for readers and calm to run for the operator—captions stay readable and timely, and going live stays under control.

## Positioning

Multilingual talk captions built for operator and phone audience together.

## Brand Personality

Simple, trustworthy, human. Voice stays plain and helpful under pressure—never corporate or technical for its own sake. Warmth lives in copy and tone, not in tinted UI surfaces. The emotional goal is quiet confidence: the room can follow, and the host can breathe.

## Anti-references

Translation-platform clutter: jargon-heavy UI, settings walls, and enterprise density that bury the live caption job. Also avoid soft warm / cream / sand body backgrounds—the product surface is restrained white.

## Design Principles

1. **Dual-surface respect** — Neither operator nor audience is secondary; each screen earns its own clarity.
2. **Clarity under pressure** — Live state stays obvious and calm when things are moving fast.
3. **Captions are the product** — Reading comfort (type, contrast, pacing) outranks decorative chrome.
4. **Hide the machinery** — Deliver translation power without settings sprawl or jargon.
5. **Restrained white** — Chroma-0 neutrals for body and chrome; color is reserved for live/state accents (`--echo-live`), not ambient warmth.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.2 AA as the bar. Preserve strong caption readability, including existing viewer contrast options and respect for reduced motion.
